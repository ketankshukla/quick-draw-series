## 🌵 The Story

**Arizona Territory, the 1870s.**

**Elijah "Quick Draw" Cade** was a Union sharpshooter. He put the war behind him, bought land, and became a peaceful rancher — until the outlaw **Rattlesnake Reno Hatch** murdered his family as one move in a corrupt railroad baron's land-grab scheme.

What begins as a white-hot quest for vengeance becomes something larger: a story about justice, love, sacrifice, and the death of the Old West itself.

Across five books, Elijah assembles an unlikely alliance and takes on an outlaw empire funded by railroad money — fighting across the mesas, canyons, and dust-choked frontier towns of the American Southwest.

> *The literary equivalent of a Sergio Leone film — gritty, cinematic, sun-scorched.*

## 🏭 The Production Pipeline

```
   Series Plan  ──▶  Chapter Outlines  ──▶  Manuscripts
                                                │
                                                ▼
   Retail Listings  ◀──  Cover Art  ◀──  Image Prompts
        │
        ▼
   EPUB + Print PDF  ──▶  Amazon KDP
```

1. **Plan** — `book-series-plan.md` fixes the arc, themes, and chapter titles before drafting
2. **Draft** — chapters written into `book-series/`, wrapped in series-specific front and back matter
3. **Prompt** — art prompts built from the templates in `book-prompts/`, tuned for character consistency
4. **Generate** — covers, wraps, portraits, and box-set art land in `book-images/`
5. **List** — titles, blurbs, descriptions, categories, and keywords assembled in `amazon-production/`
6. **Build** — EPUB and print PDF per book; manuscript DOCX via **Pandoc + PowerShell**
